General Instructions & Marking Scheme
  1. Strict Zero Negative Marking: Each correct response awards +1 mark. Unanswered or wrong questions award 0 marks. No negative marks are deducted under any circumstance.
  2. Server-Side Clock: The countdown timer at the top of your screen is synced continuously with the backend server. When time expires, your assessment is automatically submitted.
  3. Instant Autosave: Every question selection is saved immediately to the server via background AJAX. Refreshing your page or closing your browser will preserve your answers and resume at the exact remaining server time.
  4. Navigating Between Questions:
    • Click Save & Next to save your answer and proceed to the next question.
    • Click Mark for Review & Next to flag the question and proceed.
    • Click Clear Response to deselect the currently chosen option.
    • Click Previous to return to the previous question.
    • Click any question number in the Question Palette to jump directly to it.
  5. Section Navigation: You can switch between sections at any time by clicking the section tab names at the top of the exam screen.
  6. Practice-Mode Discipline Monitoring: This assessment features basic browser-level anti-cheat controls (fullscreen mode, tab-switch and window-blur tracking) designed to simulate the discipline of real corporate hiring tests like TCS NQT. If you switch tabs, minimize your window, or exit fullscreen, a warning is recorded on the server. Exiting fullscreen mode also counts as a discipline violation. On the 3rd warning, your assessment is automatically submitted. No webcams, screen recordings, or paid proctoring tools are used.
